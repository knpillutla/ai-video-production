# Alpine Nature Studio: 4K Panoramic Mountain Sanctuaries

Specialized exclusively in majestic alpine mountain vistas, Swiss Alps, Dolomites, Canadian Rockies, Matterhorn, snow-capped jagged peaks, and rolling wildflower valleys.

## Directorial Principles
- **Wide Panoramic Landscape Framing:** 16:9 panoramic vistas on a locked tripod with deep edge-to-edge optical clarity.
- **Natural Balanced Illumination:** 5500K-6000K crisp daylight, soft morning alpine glow.
- **Strict Anti-Waterfall Guard:** Zero close-up waterfall plunge walls, zero dark desaturated gray mist walls.
- **Acoustic Soundscape:** 432Hz ambient soundscape with gentle mountain breeze, distant acoustic folk strings, and crystal glacial stream murmurs.
- **Motion Diffusion:** Wan 2.1 or Hunyuan with `landscape_solid` domain; peaks and meadows remain 100% static.
