## 1. Directorial Aesthetics across 6 Diurnal Timings

1. **Inside-Out Luxury Tent Framing**:
   - 16:9 panoramic compositions on locked tripod looking from inside an ultra-luxury Bedouin glamping pavilion onto endless golden dunes.
   - Interior luxury: Hand-carved low teak table, ornate Moroccan engraved brass tea service, rich hand-woven Berber kilim rugs, plush geometric floor cushions, sheer cream linen curtains billowing at frame edges.
   - Purity Guard: Strictly zero tourists, zero vehicles, zero modern clutter, zero trash.

2. **The 6 Diurnal Atmospheric Archetypes**:
   - `desert_daytime_tent` (5500K): Crisp natural daylight, cerulean blue sky, golden ripples, sheer linen fluttering.
   - `desert_sunrise_tent` (3800K): Pastel peach & amber dawn radiance, first sun rays cresting dune ridges, delicate steam from Moroccan mint tea glass.
   - `desert_sunset_tent` (2800K): Fiery terracotta & burnt crimson golden hour, dramatic elongated purple shadows, warm brass lantern.
   - `desert_night_tent` (2000K): Deep velvet indigo sky, glittering Milky Way galaxy, single candle lantern, **strictly zero fire**.
   - `desert_campfire_hearth` (2200K): Pulsing golden embers in circular stone hearth, pierced Moroccan lanterns, starlight.
   - `desert_rain_sanctuary` (4800K): Diffused silver-slate overcast, gentle rain on canvas ASMR, damp sand ripples, cozy dry tent shelter.

3. **Culturally Grounded 432Hz Audio Matrix & Dynamic Permutation**:
   - **Sahara (Morocco/Egypt):** Resonant major-pentatonic handpan, Moroccan wooden nay flute, warm oud drone, clay water pot textures.
   - **Arabian (UAE/Saudi/Jordan):** Celestial qanun zither, Arabian nay flute, crystal singing bowls, velvet synth pads.
   - **Namib & Kalahari:** Ethereal kalimba/mbira thumb piano, resonant hang drum, warm acoustic bass drone, coastal fog breeze.
   - **Atacama (Chile):** Andean bamboo quena flute, Tibetan singing bowls, glass chimes, 432Hz delta sleep drones.
   - **American Southwest:** Native cedar courting flute, quartz crystal bowls, resonant handpan, canyon wind whisper.
   - **Anti-Monotony Guard:** Every creation permutes lead instruments and diurnal mode. Zero solo guitar, zero twang, -21 LUFS sleep master.

4. **Cinemagraph Kinetics (Wan 2.1 & Kling v1.6 Pro)**:
   - Sand dunes, furniture, brass teapot, and horizon line remain 100% frozen, rigid, and temporally stable.
   - Animate ONLY subtle sheer linen curtain flutter, delicate tea steam wisps, fine sand skimming distant crests, or gentle rain/embers.
