# Forest Studio Skill

Specialized studio skill for directing and producing 4K broadcast-grade **Ancient Temperate Forests, Komorebi Sunbeams, Mossy Boulder Brooks, and Forest Wallpapers**. Built on and strictly adhering to the [ai-video-producer-core](file:///c:/neel-1/projects/content-generation/.agents/skills/ai-video-producer-core/SKILL.md) skill.

---

## 1. Directorial Aesthetics & Archetypes

1. **Cathedral Forest Glade Framing**:
   - 16:9 panoramic compositions on locked tripod capturing towering ancient tree trunks, lush moss carpets, crystal forest brooks, and dappled sunbeams.
   - Elements: Dew-kissed ferns, weathered granite boulders, winding crystal water, ethereal mist ribbons.
   - Purity Guard: Strictly zero asphalt roads, zero hikers, zero modern markers, zero timber logging.

2. **The 4 Archetypes**:
   - `forest_mossy_canopy_day` (5400K): Crisp midday daylight illuminating dense emerald cedar canopy and crystal boulder stream.
   - `forest_babbling_brook` (5200K): Gentle forest brook cascading over mossy rock ledges through lush fern understory.
   - `forest_morning_sunbeams` (4500K): Golden morning sunbeams piercing through misty redwood canopy (komorebi lighting).
   - `forest_twilight_fireflies` (2500K): Enchanted twilight forest glade with gentle glowing fireflies dancing among ancient trees.

3. **Acoustic Mastering & Kinetics**:
   - Babbling forest brook ripples, rustling canopy leaves, morning birdsong, warm acoustic harp, 432Hz woodwind flute (-21 LUFS).
   - Ancient trunks and forest floor remain 100% stable; animate crystal water currents, subtle fern sway, and drifting mist.
