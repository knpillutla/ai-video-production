# Mountain Studio Skill

Specialized studio skill for directing and producing 4K broadcast-grade **High Alpine Peaks, Granite Spires, Glacial Ridges, and Mountain Living Wallpapers**. Built on and strictly adhering to the [ai-video-producer-core](file:///c:/neel-1/projects/content-generation/.agents/skills/ai-video-producer-core/SKILL.md) skill.

---

## 1. Directorial Aesthetics & Archetypes

1. **Panoramic Alpine Vista Framing**:
   - 16:9 panoramic compositions on locked tripod capturing sweeping alpine vistas, majestic jagged granite peaks, glacial tarns, and pristine mountain air.
   - Purity Guard: Strictly zero tourists, zero modern vehicles, zero power lines, zero hiking clutter.

2. **The 4 Diurnal Archetypes**:
   - `mountain_daytime_vista` (5500K): Crisp natural daylight, brilliant blue alpine sky, snow-capped granite spires, emerald valley below.
   - `mountain_summit_dawn` (3800K): Pastel peach & alpenglow dawn radiance illuminating mountain peaks above sea of clouds.
   - `mountain_sunset_alpenglow` (2800K): Fiery crimson & rose alpenglow bathing jagged granite ridges, deep purple valley shadows.
   - `mountain_starlit_ridge` (2000K): Deep indigo night sky, brilliant Milky Way arching over snowy spires, crystal silence.

3. **Acoustic Mastering & Cinemagraph Kinetics**:
   - High alpine wind whispers, distant glacial melt trickles, warm Tibetan singing bowls and low cello drones (-21 LUFS).
   - Solid mountains remain 100% temporally stable; animate only passing cloud wisps, summit spindrift, or water ripples.
