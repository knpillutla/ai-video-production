## 1. Directorial Aesthetics across 6 Diurnal Timings

1. **Inside-Out Coastal Sanctuary Framing**:
   - 16:9 panoramic compositions on locked tripod looking from inside an ultra-luxury coastal vantage point (shaded overwater villa deck, Mediterranean stone veranda, cliffside terrace, or beach shelter).
   - Interior luxury: Polished teak decking, sheer white/ivory linen drapes fluttering at columns, plush daybeds, artisanal chilled beverage on driftwood table.
   - Purity Guard: Strictly zero tourists, zero boats, zero modern clutter, zero trash.

2. **The 6 Diurnal Atmospheric Archetypes**:
   - `ocean_daytime_shore` (5500K): Crisp natural daylight, brilliant sun illuminating turquoise lagoon and rolling white wave swells, cerulean sky.
   - `ocean_sunrise_coast` (3800K): Pastel rose and warm amber dawn radiance, first sunbeams reflecting across glassy ocean swells, steaming morning coffee.
   - `ocean_sunset_horizon` (3000K): Fiery terracotta and molten gold sunset, long oceanic reflections, relaxing evening tide.
   - `ocean_night_bioluminescent` (2200K): Deep velvet indigo night sky, Milky Way galaxy, gentle dark waves glowing with electric-blue bioluminescence, **strictly zero fire**.
   - `ocean_campfire_hearth` (2000K): Warm driftwood embers in beach stone pit on sand, rhythmic dark ocean surf breaking in immediate background.
   - `ocean_tropical_rain` (4500K): Diffuse silver-gray overcast, soothing rain falling across calm turquoise sea, concentric ripples, sheltered teak balcony.

3. **Culturally Grounded 432Hz Audio Matrix & Dynamic Permutation**:
   - **Polynesian / Pacific (Hawaii, Tahiti):** Hawaiian slack-key acoustic guitar harmonics, soft ukulele swells, sea shell wind chimes.
   - **Mediterranean / Aegean (Santorini, Amalfi):** Greek bouzouki overtones, Spanish nylon-string classical guitar, warm azure sea breeze.
   - **Caribbean / Tropical Atolls (Maldives, Bahamas):** Resonant handpan in major pentatonic mode, wooden tongue drum, soft marimba tones.
   - **Pacific Northwest & Atlantic (Big Sur, Oregon):** Low resonant acoustic cello drone, warm fingerpicked folk guitar, misty coastal fog.
   - **Indian Ocean (Seychelles, Mauritius):** Crystal singing bowls, ethereal bamboo bansuri flute, soft concert harp arpeggios.
   - **Anti-Monotony Guard:** Every creation permutes lead instruments and diurnal mode. -21 LUFS sleep master.

4. **Rule 20 Natural Water Dynamics (Wan 2.1 & Kling v3 Pro)**:
   - Smooth laminar swells and gentle ripples auto-routed to **Alibaba Wan 2.1**.
   - Crashing surf and spray plumes routed to **Kling v3 Pro**.
   - Villa architecture, furniture, and sky remain 100% frozen, rigid, and temporally stable.
   - Animate ONLY continuous rhythmic rolling ocean wave swells, sheer linen flutter, and specular light caustics.
