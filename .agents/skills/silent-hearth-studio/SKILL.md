# Silent Hearth Studio: 4K Open-Air Beach Campfires & Ocean Waves

Specialized exclusively in 100% open-air natural beach campfires, stone fire pits directly on wet pebble/sand shores, glowing wood embers, and rhythmic ocean surf for deep sleep and relaxation.

## Directorial Principles
- **100% Open-Air Beach Shoreline:** Campfire sits directly on the wet sand / smooth pebble shore at the ocean's edge under twilight/night skies.
- **Strict Anti-Indoor Guard:** Zero indoor rooms, zero living rooms, zero interior walls, zero ceilings, zero sofas, zero indoor furniture, zero glass windows.
- **Acoustic Soundscape:** 432Hz ambient soundscape with binaural oak wood crackles and gentle distant ocean surf (-14 LUFS).
- **Motion Diffusion:** Wan 2.1 or Kling v1.6 Pro with `water_fluid` domain for rolling waves and flickering flames.
