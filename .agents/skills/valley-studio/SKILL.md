# Valley Studio Skill

Specialized studio skill for directing and producing 4K broadcast-grade **Pastoral Valleys, Wildflower Meadows, Glacial Meanders, and Tranquil Countryside Wallpapers**. Built on and strictly adhering to the [ai-video-producer-core](file:///c:/neel-1/projects/content-generation/.agents/skills/ai-video-producer-core/SKILL.md) skill.

---

## 1. Directorial Aesthetics & Archetypes

1. **Pastoral Alpine Valley Framing**:
   - 16:9 panoramic compositions on locked tripod capturing lush valley floors blooming with alpine flora, winding glacial brooks, and rustic wooden timber barns.
   - Purity Guard: Strictly zero asphalt roads, zero modern vehicles, zero plastic clutter.

2. **The 4 Diurnal Archetypes**:
   - `valley_wildflower_meadow` (5400K): Crisp midday sun illuminating blooming lupines, poppies, and buttercups with gentle brook.
   - `valley_morning_mist` (4200K): Ethereal dawn mist ribbons weaving across emerald pastures, golden sunbeams piercing morning air.
   - `valley_glacial_stream` (5500K): Crystal turquoise glacial stream babbling over rounded river stones through alpine meadows.
   - `valley_sunset_pastoral` (3000K): Warm golden hour light sweeping across pastoral valley slopes, long amber shadows.

3. **Acoustic Mastering & Kinetics**:
   - Gentle babbling glacial stream water ASMR, meadow grass whispers, birdsong, warm acoustic harp and wooden flute (-21 LUFS).
   - Solid valley terrain remains locked; animate only subtle flower swaying, crystal stream currents, and drifting mist.
