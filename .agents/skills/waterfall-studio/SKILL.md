# Waterfall Studio: 4K Monumental Cascades & Roaring Cataracts

Specialized exclusively in colossal plunging waterfalls, world-famous cataracts (Niagara, Victoria, Iguazu), and dense spray mist soundscapes.

## Directorial Principles
- **Eye-Level Wall-to-Wall Framing:** Directly confront the plunging cataract water curtain spanning 100% of the horizontal screen.
- **Atmospheric Isolation:** Dense billowing mist rising from the turquoise plunge pool.
- **Acoustic Soundscape:** 432Hz ambient brown noise with continuous deep bass water roaring foley.
- **Motion Diffusion:** Wan 2.1 or Kling v1.6 Pro with `water_fluid` domain; cliffs remain 100% static.
