1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & STRATEGIC DECISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Nations & Civilizations studio taxonomy generates deep, episodic documentary
content across 7 continents, 195+ countries, and 22 thematic archetypes (history,
architecture, spirituality, gastronomy, geology, regional zones, etc.).

Publishing this content posed a strategic distribution dilemma:
1. The 195-Country Channel Anti-Pattern: Creating a separate YouTube channel for
   each country (e.g. Poland Discovery, Italy Discovery, Vietnam Discovery) divides
   audiences and creates an insurmountable barrier to monetization: every channel
   would need its own 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours, while managing 195
   accounts violates YouTube verification and API quota limits.
2. The Monolithic Mega-Channel: Putting all global content on a single generic channel
   dilutes audience focus and weakens regional algorithmic affinity.

The Approved Decision: Continental Hub Network (Model A)
We consolidate production into 4 to 5 regional continental hub channels, led
immediately by the user's active channel: Euro Discovery.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHY CONTINENTAL HUBS WIN ON YOUTUBE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Compounding Algorithmic Affinity:
   Viewers interested in European history, alpine nature, or medieval architecture
   subscribe to Euro Discovery. When they watch a video on Poland, YouTube's
   recommendation engine immediately serves them your Austria, Italy, and Greece
   episodes on the same channel, maximizing binge sessions and channel session time.

2. Rapid YPP Monetization (1,000 Subs / 4,000 Watch Hours):
   Instead of fragmenting views across 25 separate European channels, all watch
   hours pool into Euro Discovery, accelerating qualification for the YouTube
   Partner Program and maximizing ad RPM.

3. Account & API Quota Safety:
   Managing 4-5 continental channels is 100% compliant with standard Google Brand
   Accounts and YouTube Data API v3 quotas, eliminating phone verification
   headaches and bot flags.
