Page 1: Quick Reference & Costs

The 3-Camera Permanence Secret

Eliminates the "AI slideshow" look by locking into 3 recurring setups: Cam A (Close-Up), Cam B (Medium 45°), and Cam C (Contemplative B-Roll).

Method 1: Head-Turn Extraction

1 master FLUX hero still → Kling 5s 3D rotation → 12 extracted stills. Guarantees 100% identical facial geometry with zero drift.

Broadcast 4K Delivery Standard

Lanczos-3 sinc upscaling, unsharp contrast mask, Rec.709 color tags, -14 LUFS loudness, and 48kHz 320k AAC audio.

End-to-End Execution Sequence Flow
1. Hero Face (FLUX 8K) → 2. 3D Rotation (Kling I2V) → 3. 12 Stills (FFmpeg) → 4. Train LoRA (Fal.ai) → 5. Song & Lyrics (Gemini+Suno) → 6. Vocal Guide (FFmpeg) → 7. 3 Shots (FLUX LoRA) → 8. Lip-Sync (LivePortrait) → 9. B-Roll Motion (Kling I2V) → 10. Hook Punch-In (FFmpeg) → 11. Multi-Cam EDL (BeatDetector) → 12. 4K Master (FFmpeg Lanczos-3)

Step 10 Broadcast 4K Master Engine Single-Pass FFmpeg Master
scale=3840:2160:flags=lanczos
  • Lanczos-3 Sinc Upscaling: flags=lanczos preserves crisp edge contrast.
  • Unsharp Mask: unsharp=5:5:0.8:5:5:0.0 restores micro-skin pores.
  • Visually Lossless: libx264, CRF 22, yuv420p.
  • Broadcast Color: Rec.709 tags prevent washed-out blacks or grey skin on YouTube.
  • EBU R128 Loudness: -af loudnorm=I=-14.0:LRA=7.0:TP=-1.0 prevents YouTube volume-ducking.
  • Broadcast Audio: 48kHz 320k AAC stereo with +faststart.
