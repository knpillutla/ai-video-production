The way I would actually build it

Don't ask either tool:

"Build my entire AI video platform."

That's likely to create a huge amount of code before you've validated the architecture.

Instead, give Codex your updated V2 architecture and make it build vertical slices.

Phase 1 — Foundation

Have Codex build:

FastAPI
PostgreSQL
Redis / queue
Docker Compose
project management
configuration
logging
telemetry
provider interfaces
basic web UI

Then test it.

Phase 2 — First actual video

This is the most important phase.

Build only:

Topic
 ↓
Research packet
 ↓
Original script
 ↓
Scene plan
 ↓
Image generation
 ↓
TTS
 ↓
Music/SFX
 ↓
Remotion + FFmpeg
 ↓
Captions
 ↓
Final MP4
 ↓
QA
 ↓
Human approval

Don't build 30 model integrations yet.

Get one 8–12 minute video reliably produced.

Phase 3 — Rights + monetization readiness

Then implement the parts I added to your V2:

Asset
 ↓
Rights Ledger
 ↓
License verification
 ↓
Originality/risk analysis
 ↓
Synthetic-media assessment
 ↓
Final Video QA
 ↓
Publish Gate

This is one of the most important improvements to your original architecture.
