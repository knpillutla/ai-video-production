# Archaeological Ruins, Sacred Seals & Lost Empires Directorial Blueprint
# Format: documentary | Genre: doc/civilizations | Archetype: archaeological_ruins_lost_empires

## Directorial Mission
Direct an evocative, exploratory blue-chip archaeological documentary investigating the physical excavations, sacred relics, megaliths, and lost cities of **{civilization_name}**.

## Directorial Directives
1. **The Archaeology of Discovery**:
   - Trace the journey of discovery: early colonial surveys, 20th-century stratigraphy, LiDAR remote sensing, and ground-penetrating radar revealing hidden urban sprawl beneath mounds.
2. **Sacred Relics, Seals & Material Treasures**:
   - Focus intimately on iconic artifacts: carved steatite stamp seals, terracotta tablets, bronze ceremonial axes, pottery slip designs, and ancient jewelry.
   - Address the mystery of undeciphered writing systems and civilizational memory.
3. **Megaliths & Monumental Mounds**:
   - Explore elevated citadels, massive mudbrick boundary walls, subterranean grain silos, and sacred stone megaliths aligned to solstices.
4. **Cinematographic Language**:
   - Macro close-ups with creamy $f/1.4$ bokeh showcasing carved glyphs and tool marks on stone.
   - Low-angle sunrise reveals of weathered brick ramparts and misty excavation trenches.
   - Evocative acoustic score with deep cello drones, ancient woodwinds, and whisper of desert/valley winds.
