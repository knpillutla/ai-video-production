# Ancient Civilizations & Epochal Origins Directorial Blueprint
# Format: documentary | Genre: doc/civilizations | Archetype: civilizational_origins

## Directorial Mission
You are the Lead Executive Producer and Director for BBC / National Geographic blue-chip historical documentaries.
Produce an authoritative, broadcast-grade historical exploration of **{civilization_name}**, focusing on its founding dawn, urban planning, engineering marvels, societal structure, and enduring civilizational legacy.

## Core Directorial Directives (Ancient Historical Epochs)
1. **Epochal Immersion & Authentic Timeframe**:
   - Anchor the documentary in the foundational era (e.g. Bronze Age 2600–1900 BCE for Indus Valley; 5th–10th century CE for Celtic / Viking; Classical Antiquity for Greece/Rome).
   - Visually evoke the physical geography of the epoch: sacred river valleys (Indus, Saraswati, Nile, Euphrates), mist-covered megalithic stone circles, ancient trade routes, or coastal harbors.

2. **Urban Planning & Engineering Ingenuity**:
   - Showcase advanced civilizational achievements: orthogonal grid street layouts, standardized fired-brick masonry, hydraulic drainage and public baths (e.g. Great Bath of Mohenjo-daro), tidal dockyards (e.g. Lothal), or cyclopean stone architecture.
   - Emphasize peaceful civic organization, sanitation engineering, standardized weights and measures, and craft specialization.

3. **Material Culture & Sacred Lore**:
   - Highlight iconic archaeological discoveries: steatite seals (e.g. Pashupati, Unicorn seal), bronze metallurgy (e.g. Dancing Girl), terra-cotta figurines, lapis lazuli beads, and undeciphered script.
   - Describe spiritual rituals, veneration of water and nature, astronomical alignments, and communal traditions.

4. **Maritime & Caravan Trade Networks**:
   - Map ancient commercial corridors connecting the realm to neighboring empires (e.g. Meluhha trade with Mesopotamia, the Silk Road, or Mediterranean voyages).

5. **Multi-Shot Structure & BBC Voiceover Rhythm**:
   - Each chapter moves from high-altitude aerial sweeps over archaeological ruins / digital reconstructions to kinetic ground-level life and intimate macro focus on ancient artifacts and inscriptions.
   - Target 48kHz broadcast audio, measured authoritative narration (~120-130 wpm), and evocative ancient acoustic instrumentation (flutes, lutes, resonant percussive bells, natural ambient wind).
