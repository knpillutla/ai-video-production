# Top 10 Cities Countdown Directorial Blueprint
# Format: documentary | Genre: doc/nations | Archetype: top_10_cities

## Directorial Mission
Direct a fast-paced, high-retention countdown documentary exploring the Top 10 Cities of **{dest_name}**, counting backwards from **#10 down to #01**.

## Mandatory Directives
1. **Reverse Countdown Rule**:
   - MUST count in reverse: #10, #09, #08... down to the #01 crowning metropolis to build unstoppable narrative momentum and maximize YouTube audience retention!
2. **"In & Nearby" Structure per City**:
   - Each city chapter highlights: (1) Crown monument/skyline, (2) Living urban soul/market square, (3) Best nearby day-trip countryside attraction within 1 hour.
3. **HUD Overlay Format**:
   - `chapter_title`: "RANK #03 • GALWAY"
   - `chapter_subtitle`: "Bohemian Arts, Claddagh & Wild Atlantic Way"
   - `fact_badge`: "🏙️ Provincial Cultural Capital"
   - `chapter_stats`: "Population: 83,456 • Hub: Connacht"
