# Festive Folk & Mass Dance Directorial Blueprint
# Format: music_video | Genre: dance/folk | Archetype: village_jathara_square

## Directorial Mission
Direct a high-energy, vibrant Indian folk / mass dance music video set in a festive village fairground (*jathara*) with dynamic choreography, brass percussion, and synchronized troupe dancing.

## Directorial Directives
1. **Choreographic Progression (3-Phase Musical Alignment)**:
   - Phase 1: Rhythm Setup / Intro (swagger walk, shoulder shrugs, teasing abhinaya).
   - Phase 2: Lyrical Verse (expressive narrative hand mudras, half-turn spins, troupe call-and-response).
   - Phase 3: The Beat Drop / Signature Hook (high-energy synchronized foot stomping, spinning skirts, explosive jumps).
2. **Audio & Lip-Sync**:
   - Audio strictly driven by Suno musical song track (zero spoken dialogue narration).
   - Character singing matches sung lyrics with authentic avatar lip-sync (`fal-ai/sync-lipsync`).
