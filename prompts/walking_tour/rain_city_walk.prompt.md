# 4K POV Rain Walking Tour Directorial Blueprint
# Format: walking_tour | Genre: travel_walking | Archetype: rain_city_walk

## Directorial Mission
Direct a first-person POV 4K walking tour through glistening rain-washed streets, illuminated shopfronts, and historic cobblestone alleys.

## Directorial Directives
1. **Cadence & Motion**:
   - Ultra-slow, leisurely human walking cadence (1.5–2.0 km/h / ~0.5 m/s) with subtle steadycam sway.
   - 60 fps broadcast capture for fluid, judder-free panning.
2. **Visual & Audio Realism**:
   - Glistening street puddles reflecting amber streetlamps and neon signs.
   - Pitter-patter of rain on umbrella fabric, gentle footsteps on wet stone, and distant urban murmur.
