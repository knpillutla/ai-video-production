# Hindi Family Party Dance - Production Instruction File

## Purpose
This is a reusable scene-specific instruction file for generating a polished, high-quality Hindi family party dance video with family members and friends in a warm, joyful, festival-like setting. The goal is cinematic realism, natural emotion, rich cultural styling, and clean family-friendly composition.

## Governing Project Rules
Use the project-wide standards from [AGENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md](../AGENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md) and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), especially:
- Natural daylight and warm festival lighting
- Contextual, scene-matched clothing and jewelry
- Family-safe, advertiser-friendly content
- High-quality cinematic composition and motion
- Realistic, emotionally expressive human movement
- 4K UHD output, smooth motion, pleasant exposure, balanced color

## Core Creative Direction
Generate a Hindi family party dance scene where multiple generations of family and friends celebrate together at a festive gathering. The mood must feel warm, joyful, inclusive, lively, and culturally rooted while staying polished and premium.

### Scene Theme
- Hindi family celebration / birthday / festival gathering / reunion party
- Family and close friends dancing together in a home courtyard, terrace, living room, or decorated party venue
- Energetic but graceful choreography with affectionate family interactions
- A sense of celebration, togetherness, laughter, and real human warmth

### People Composition
- Include mixed-age family members: parents, siblings, cousins, grandparents, young adults, children
- Include at least 6-15 people so the frame feels alive and celebratory
- Ensure faces are visible and expressions are happy, natural, and welcoming
- Avoid stiff poses; encourage natural smiles, hand claps, shoulder movements, and group dancing
- Limit weird or overly exaggerated expressions; aim for natural, relatable joy

## Visual Style Guidelines
### Camera and Framing
- Use cinematic but intimate framing
- Wide shots to show full family dance group
- Medium shots to capture joyful expressions and dance motion
- Close-ups on hands, feet, jewelry, smiles, and emotional moments
- Stable handheld motion with subtle movement, not jittery or robotic
- Use natural motion transitions between wide and close framing
- Avoid extreme zooming, chaotic camera movement, or over-stylized unnatural angles

### Motion and Dance Direction
- Dance should be energetic, clean, and humanly believable
- Include coordinated group movement, claps, spins, shoulder sways, foot taps, hand gestures, and playful laughter
- Blend classic Indian festive dance energy with modern family-party choreography
- Keep movement expressive, not stiff or overly synthetic
- Background performers should also move rhythmically to make the scene feel alive

### Lighting
- Preferred lighting: warm daylight with soft festive fill light
- Indoor scenes: soft diffused light from windows, warm bulbs, fairy lights, and ambient chandeliers
- Outdoor scenes: natural daylight or golden-hour but controlled, not oversaturated or harsh
- Keep skin tones natural and healthy
- Avoid artificial yellow wash, dark muddy shadows, or impossible harsh highlights
- Use soft, flattering highlights on faces and clothing
- For night or evening scenes, warm white lights, lanterns, fairy lights, and subtle bokeh around the family

### Color and Environment
- Rich festive palette: maroon, royal blue, emerald green, mustard yellow, pink, saffron, ivory, red, gold, teal, and soft pastel accents
- Use decorative elements such as marigold garlands, lanterns, balloons, curtain drapes, flowers, colorful chairs, and party props
- Background should feel celebratory and lived-in, not empty or sterile
- The environment should suggest a family home, terrace, courtyard, banquet hall, or decorated community venue

## Clothing and Wardrobe
### Women
- Elegant festive Indian outfits: sarees, lehenga choli, anarkali suits, sharara sets, festive salwar suits
- Traditional colors: royal red, deep maroon, teal, mustard, pink, emerald, gold accents, ivory, lavender
- Light embroidery, zari work, gota patti, mirror work, sequins, thread work
- Comfortable but rich festive silhouettes with movement in mind
- Soft dupattas and flowing fabric in dance scenes
- Avoid over-glossy synthetic-looking fabric; use rich, natural fabric textures

### Men
- Traditional festive wear: kurta-pajama, Nehru jackets, sherwani, bandhgalas, embroidered waistcoats
- Colors: ivory, navy, maroon, olive green, rust, beige, royal blue, mustard, charcoal
- Neatly groomed look with polished but not stiff styling
- Some men can wear ethnic jackets with contrast kurtas for family dance vibe

### Children and Teens
- Colorful festive clothes with comfortable movement
- Kids: bright kurta sets, lehengas, ethnic dresses, playful color-block outfits
- Teens: modern Indian festive wear with stylish fusion elements

### Footwear and Accessories
- Traditional festive shoes, mojari, kolhapuri, embroidered flats, dress shoes
- Sandals or elegant comfortable dance footwear
- Keep accessories graceful and culturally fitting

## Jewelry and Beauty
### Jewelry Requirements
- Women: gold jhumkas, bangles, kada, necklaces, maang tikka, waist chains, anklets, temple jewelry, kundan, polki, statement earrings
- Men: subtle gold or silver rings, bracelets, pocket watches if appropriate, classic ethnic accessories
- Family-friendly decorative pieces: modest, elegant, festive, and matching the outfit colors
- Jewelry should be visible in close-ups and mid-shots for richness and authenticity

### Beauty and Grooming
- Natural, radiant skin tones
- Soft festive makeup: glowing skin, defined eyes, bindi, light eyeliner, modest blush, warm lipstick tones
- Groomed hair styled with traditional touches or elegant modern fashion
- Men with neat hair and clean facial styling
- Avoid over-processed or plastic-looking skin

## Background Examples
Use one or more of the following backgrounds:
- Decorated family living room with festive curtains and floral arrangements
- Open terrace or courtyard with fairy lights and colorful cloth drapes
- Banquet hall or wedding-style family celebration venue with stage-like setup
- Home garden or backyard with string lights and marigold flowers
- Spacious festive indoor hall with elegant Indian decor, lanterns, and family portraits

### Decor Details
- Marigold garlands, diya lights, lanterns, floral backdrops, colorful drapes
- Festive balloons and metallic accents
- Traditional Indian party table with sweets, flowers, and family decor
- Warm ambient lights and soft bokeh in the background
- Keep the environment rich but not cluttered or chaotic

## Audio and Music Direction
- Use joyful Hindi or Indian family-party music with vibrant rhythm
- Include dhol, tabla, percussion, folk instruments, modern beats, or celebratory Bollywood-inspired dance rhythm
- Music should enhance the dance without overpowering the emotional feeling
- No spoken narration or harsh voiceover in the main dance sequence
- Keep audio clean, lively, and polished

## Composition Rules
- Family members should feel connected and comfortable with one another
- Include group energy: laughter, clapping, cheering, hand-holding, spinning, and friendly interaction
- Ensure no one looks isolated or awkwardly placed in the frame
- Keep the group balanced across the composition, with natural spacing between people
- Avoid empty corners, cut-off limbs, or distorted body proportions
- Strong depth and visual rhythm through layering of people and decor

## Safety and Quality Standards
- Family-safe, inclusive, respectful, culturally appropriate
- No unsafe gestures, no inappropriate wardrobe, no exaggerated or vulgar dance movement
- Avoid over-saturation, digital artifacting, unnatural skin, or cheap AI-looking faces
- Output should look like a premium real-family celebration video, not an artificial, plastic, filtered montage
- Keep the final result elegant, warm, vibrant, and emotionally authentic

## Preferred Output Spec
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 9:16 depending on platform
- Resolution: 4K UHD preferred
- Frame rate: 30 fps for dance scenes
- Lighting: natural + warm festive fill
- Color grade: rich, warm, healthy, celebratory
- Visual tone: joyful, premium, cinematic, authentic
