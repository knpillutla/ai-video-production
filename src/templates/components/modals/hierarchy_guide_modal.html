1. Format (The Content Medium & Pacing Contract)

The top-level production framework determining audio mastering, frame rate, and narration:

🎬 Documentary: 24 fps cinematic pacing, voiceover narration, verified facts, educational HUD stats.
🧘 Relaxation: 24 fps serene drifts, 432Hz acoustic music + pure nature ASMR dual masters.
🚶 Walking Tour: 60 fps ultra-slow human walking cadence (1.5-2.0 km/h) with steadycam sway.
🎵 Music Video: 30 fps beat-driven choreography, Suno song stems & avatar lip-sync.
2. Genre (The Thematic Category)

The primary subject category of the production. For documentaries:

🌍 doc/nations (Sovereign Nations):

For single modern countries (e.g. Ireland, Norway, India, Japan, France). Explores sovereign governance, capitals, geography, society, universities, economy, and living culture.

🏺 doc/civilizations (Ancient Civilizations):

For trans-national ancient realms, lost empires, and founding historical epochs that transcend modern borders (e.g. Indus Valley, Celtic World, Viking Realm, Silk Road, Greco-Roman Antiquity).

3. Sub-Genre (Regional Sphere & Cultural Scope)

Refines the thematic category to a specific regional, geographical, or cultural sphere:

🏺 indus_vedic:

Indus Valley civilization, Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Saraswati river basin, Lothal dockyards, and early Vedic epochs.

☘️ celtic_world:

Celtic and Gaelic civilizations across Ireland, Scotland, Wales, Cornwall, and Brittany.

🌍 europe_nations:

European democracies, alpine republics, and Atlantic maritime nations.

4. Archetype (Chapter Blueprint & Mapped Prompt Files)

Each archetype in format_taxonomy.json maps to two dedicated prompt files: (1) Story Prompt File (governs narrative chapters, real-world educational facts, badges, and narration) and (2) Screenplay Prompt File (governs camera angles, 1:1 show-and-tell synchrony, Ken Burns motion, and diffusion prompts).
