CineAI Studio Production Guide

Understanding Formats, Genres, Biomes, and Weather Controls

Level 1: Format (The Content Medium)

Defines the production engine, pacing contract, audio mastering, and camera cadence:

🦅 Documentary: BBC/NatGeo style with authoritative voiceover, 4-scene narrative arc, 24fps cinema cadence, and active 4K perspective drone choreography (2s–14s).
🧘 Relaxation & Ambient: Living wallpaper soundscape, single locked perspective (zero drift), 432Hz acoustic pads, ASMR natural audio, and 0% voiceover.
🚶 4K Walking Tour: First-person POV steadycam at human walking pace (~1.5–2.0 km/h) with natural footstep sway and binaural street audio.
💃 Musical Dance & Folk: Beat-driven choreographic progression, Suno vocal arrangement, synchronized lip-sync, and 30fps dynamic footwork.
Level 2: Sub-Genre (Subject Matter & Biome)

Specifies the environmental domain, physical materials, and lighting characteristics:

🏔️ Mountains & Alpines: Glacial peaks, granite faces, alpine meadows, and valleys.
🌊 Oceans & Marine: Coral reefs, crystalline lagoons, abyssal midnight trenches.
🏖️ Coastal & Beaches: Tropical white sand, volcanic black sand, sea stacks, cabanas.
🌲 Forests & Canopies: Redwood giants, mossy temperate rainforests, bamboo groves.
🏜️ Deserts & Dunes: Colossal star dunes, canyon chasms, Bedouin desert hearths.
🏛️ Cities & Heritage: Historic old towns, ancient Egyptian pyramids, stone fortresses.
Level 3: Archetype (Setting Template & Landmark)

Picks a curated real-world location template (e.g. Swiss Alps - Lauterbrunnen Valley, Whitsundays - Whitehaven Beach, Sahara Grand Erg Dunes), pre-populating historical lore and visual prompts.

Level 4: Weather & Atmosphere (Procedural Dynamic FX)

Explicit physical weather engine applied to the scene without changing the underlying biome:

☀️ Normal: Crisp 5400K natural daylight, clean air, balanced skin/rock tones.
🌧️ Rain: 3-layer translucent rain, water ripples, mist layer & petrichor ASMR.
❄️ Snow: Aerodynamic drifting flakes, wind tilt, frosted pines & winter breeze.
🌫️ Mist/Fog: Soft volumetric god rays, morning mountain cloud layers.
🌅 Sunset: Warm 3200K amber alpenglow, long purple shadows across terrain.
🌌 Starlit: Deep indigo sky, glittering Milky Way galaxy & night stillness.
